Plan Pithoragarh Expedition
Tier 1 Flagship Region • Frontier Himalayas

Pithoragarh — Gateway to the High Himalayas

Standing face-to-face with the five snow-capped peaks of Panchachuli, the sacred ॐ of Om Parvat, Parvati Kund at Adi Kailash, and wild glacial frontiers.

Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Munsiyari & Panchachuli Permit & Fitness Advisory
Altitude & Acclimatization

Ranges from 1,600m to 4,497m (Parvati Kund). Gradual ascent via Dharchula & Gunji ensures safe acclimatization.

Permits (ILP)

Inner Line Permits issued by SDM Dharchula required for Byans & Darma valleys. Sadhya team assists full paperwork.

Road & 4x4 Transit

BRO motorable mountain highway through Tawaghat & Lakhanpur. Operated exclusively via licensed 4x4 mountain vehicles.

Medical & Physical Fitness

Physician fitness certificate required for high border sectors. Emergency oxygen cylinders carried on all high expeditions.

Verified High Himalayan Valleys

Frontier Valleys & Mountain Havens

From the saucer-shaped Sor valley of Pithoragarh town to the remote Rung tribal settlements bordering Tibet and Nepal.

Altitude: 2,200 m

Munsiyari & Panchachuli

The crown jewel of Pithoragarh. Direct views of Panchachuli I–V (6,904m), 126m Birthi Waterfall, Khaliya Top bugyal trek, Darkot Pashmina village, and Johar Valley gateway.

Altitude: 4,497 m

Adi Kailash & Om Parvat

Sacred Byans Valley borderland. Marvel at the natural snow ॐ on Om Parvat, perform puja at emerald Parvati Kund, and stay in Nabi, Gunji, and Kuti tribal homestays.

Altitude: 3,300 m – 4,260 m

Darma Valley & Glaciers

Untamed Dhauli Ganga canyon, Nagling & Baling cascades, traditional carved wooden houses of Dantu and Dugtu, and trek to Panchachuli Base Camp (East face).
