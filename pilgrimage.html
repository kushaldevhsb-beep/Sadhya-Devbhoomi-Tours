Kainchi Dham Ashram & Kshipra River

Nestled in the lush pine forests between two scissor-shaped hills

The Divine Presence of Maharaj-ji

Established on 15 June 1964 by the revered mystic saint Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj (lovingly called Maharaj-ji), Kainchi Dham is located just 18 km from Nainital on the Bhowali-Almora highway at an elevation of 1,400 meters. The ashram is dedicated to Lord Hanuman, and Baba Neem Karoli is himself revered by devotees as an incarnation of Pawanputra Hanuman.

The name 'Kainchi' comes from two sharp hairpin hills resembling a pair of scissors between which the ashram is blissfully sheltered, with the tranquil waters of the Kshipra river murmuring right beside the temple courtyard.

"Love everyone, serve everyone, feed everyone, remember God." — The Eternal Teaching of Baba Neem Karoli

Unlike conventional pilgrimage places, Kainchi Dham radiates an unmistakable, profound stillness. Devotees from every walk of life sit quietly for hours reciting Hanuman Chalisa, offering prayers at Baba's sacred Samadhi Mandir, and partaking in the simple, loving prasad.

🌍

Global Spiritual Magnet: The Steve Jobs & Silicon Valley Connection

In 1974, a young Steve Jobs travelled to India seeking spiritual enlightenment and journeyed all the way up to Kainchi Dham. The spiritual awakening he experienced here deeply shaped his lifelong vision and philosophy for Apple. Decades later in 2008, when Facebook was facing critical crossroads, Steve Jobs advised Mark Zuckerberg to visit Kainchi Dham to reconnect with his deeper mission. Zuckerberg spent several days at the ashram meditating and later spoke publicly about how that visit restored his clarity.

Other global figures, including Hollywood actress Julia Roberts, humanitarian Larry Brilliant, spiritual teacher Ram Dass (author of 'Be Here Now'), and kirtan singer Krishna Das, have all traced their spiritual transformation to the divine grace of Baba Neem Karoli at Kainchi Dham.

15th June Annual Bhandara

The Pratishtha Divas (Foundation Day) on June 15 is the most celebrated festival. Over 2 to 3 lakh devotees arrive from across India and the globe. The miraculous, sacred Malpua Prasad is distributed without end throughout the day.

The 5 Sacred Shiva Shrines

Panch Kedar Circuit

According to sacred tradition, after the Kurukshetra war, the Pandavas sought Lord Shiva's pardon. Mahadeva took the form of a bull (Nandi) and dove into the earth. His divine body parts appeared across five sanctified shrines in the Garhwal Himalayas:

1. Kedarnath The Hump (Jyotirlinga) Altitude: 3,583 m • Mandakini Valley
2. Tungnath The Arms (Highest Temple) Altitude: 3,680 m • Above Chopta
3. Rudranath The Face (Mukharvind) Altitude: 2,286 m • Alpine Meadows
4. Madmaheshwar The Navel (Nabhi) Altitude: 3,497 m • Chaukhamba base
5. Kalpeshwar The Locks (Jata) Altitude: 2,200 m • Open year-round
124 Stone Temples • Almora

Jageshwar Mahadev Dham

Nestled in a narrow valley enveloped by centuries-old giant deodar cedar trees, Jageshwar comprises a cluster of 124 exquisitely carved stone temples dating from the 7th to 12th century (Katyuri and Chand dynasties). Believed to be one of the original 12 Jyotirlingas (Nageshvara), it offers unmatched spiritual quietude and authentic Vedic puja rituals.

Highlights: Maha Mrityunjaya Temple, Dandeshwar Temple, Jageshwar Archaeological Museum

Distance: 36 km from Almora, 85 km from Nainital

Geomagnetic Power Spot • Crank's Ridge

Kasar Devi Temple

Positioned on a high ridge overlooking 300 km of snow peaks, Kasar Devi is globally famous as one of only three places on Earth endowed with enormous geomagnetic energy (under the Van Allen Radiation Belt — along with Stonehenge in the UK and Machu Picchu in Peru). Swami Vivekananda meditated in its caves in 1890, followed by Lama Govinda, Bob Dylan, and George Harrison.

Spiritual Experience: Deep meditation, panoramic sunset views of Nanda Devi

Distance: 8 km from Almora town

The 5 Sacred River Confluences

Panch Prayag (Birth of Holy Ganga)

The five sacred confluences along the Alaknanda river, descending from the glaciers of Badrinath down to Devprayag where the river officially becomes the sacred Ganga:

1. Vishnuprayag Alaknanda + Dhauliganga
2. Nandaprayag Alaknanda + Nandakini
3. Karnaprayag Alaknanda + Pindar River
4. Rudraprayag Alaknanda + Mandakini
5. Devprayag Alaknanda + Bhagirathi (Birth of Ganga)
