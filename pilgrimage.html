The 5 Sacred Shiva Shrines

Panch Kedar Circuit

According to Hindu epics, after the Kurukshetra war, the Pandavas sought Lord Shiva's pardon. Lord Shiva took the form of a bull (Nandi) and dove into the earth. His body parts appeared in five sanctified shrines in the Garhwal Himalayas:

1. Kedarnath The Hump (Jyotirlinga) • 3,583m
2. Tungnath The Arms (Highest Temple) • 3,680m
3. Rudranath The Face (Mukharvind) • 2,286m
4. Madmaheshwar The Navel (Nabhi) • 3,497m
5. Kalpeshwar The Hair (Jata) • 2,200m
The 5 Sacred Confluences

Panch Prayag (Sacred River Confluences)

The five holy confluences along the Alaknanda river, descending from the glaciers of Badrinath down to Devprayag where the river officially becomes the sacred Ganga:

1. Vishnuprayag Alaknanda + Dhauliganga
2. Nandaprayag Alaknanda + Nandakini
3. Karnaprayag Alaknanda + Pindar River
4. Rudraprayag Alaknanda + Mandakini
5. Devprayag Alaknanda + Bhagirathi (Birth of Ganga)
