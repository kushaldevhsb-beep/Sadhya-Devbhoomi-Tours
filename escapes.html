Slow Living in High Altitudes

Himalayan Escapes

Breathe crisp deodar air, wake up to golden alpenglow on 7,000m snow peaks, walk through wild apple orchards, and experience serene mountain quietude.

Snow Peaks & Alpine Meadows

Munsiyari & Khaliya Top

Direct frontline view of the five sacred Panchachuli peaks (6,904m). Stay in traditional Shauka stone cottages, hike to Khaliya Top meadow, and watch sunset alpenglow.

Fruit Orchards & Cliffs

Mukteshwar & Sitla Ridge

Perched at 2,286m with 180° view of Nanda Devi. Walk through apple, plum, and apricot orchards, stand over Chauli Ki Jali cliffs, and swim in Bhalugaad pool.

300 km Himalayan Horizon

Binsar Forest Sanctuary

Dense oak and rhododendron forest reserve. Binsar Zero Point offers a jaw-dropping 300 km panorama spanning Kedarnath, Chaukhamba, Trishul, and Nanda Devi.
