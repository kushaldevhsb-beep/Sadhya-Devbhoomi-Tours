Sacred Geography

Temples, Ashrams & Spiritual Places Around Nainital

From ancient Shakti Peeths to world-revered ashrams and Kumaoni shrines of justice. We clearly categorize each site with authentic background.

Ashram & Samadhi Mandir

Kainchi Dham Ashram

Founded by mystic saint Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj in 1962 along Kshipra river. Known globally for peaceful meditation and sacred Bhandara.

Location: 18 km from Nainital on NH 109
Shakti Peeth Temple

Maa Naina Devi Temple

Perched on the northern shore of Naini Lake. Legend holds that Goddess Sati's eyes (Naina) fell here, giving the lake and town its sacred name.

Location: Mallital, Naini Lake Promenade
Temple of Justice

Ghorakhal Golu Devta

Presiding deity of justice in Kumaoni folklore. Devotees tie brass bells and submit written petitions when seeking divine resolution.

Location: 15 km from Nainital near Bhowali
Ancient Shiva Shrine

Mukteshwar Mahadev

350-year-old Shiva temple at 2,286m altitude with panoramic views of Nanda Devi. The spot where Lord Shiva granted liberation (Moksha) to a demon.

Location: 51 km from Nainital on high ridge
Spiritual Centre & Sunset

Hanuman Garhi

Built by renowned saint Baba Neem Karoli in 1950, featuring a 12-foot statue of Lord Hanuman ripping his chest to reveal Lord Rama and Sita.

Location: 3.5 km south of Tallital
Spiritual Ashram

Haidakhan Ashram

Peaceful Himalayan retreat and temple complex dedicated to Haidakhan Babaji, teaching truth, simplicity, and love along the Gautama Ganga river.

Location: Chilyanaula / Kathgodam-Ranikhet belt
Sacred Hilltop Shrine

Chota Kailash (Pinro)

Revered mountain sanctuary dedicated to Lord Shiva near Bhimtal with scenic trekking trails through dense pine woods.

Location: 12 km from Bhimtal
Heritage Temple

Bhowali Golu Devta

Historic temple in the sanatorium pine belt of Bhowali, coupled with organic tea gardens and fruit orchards.

Location: Bhowali Town Junction
Natural Wonders

Lakes, Waterfalls, Ridges & Forests

Experience the incredible biodiversity of the Kumaon foothills from serene lakes to dense oak-rhododendron canopies.

Bhimtal, Sattal & Naukuchiatal

The pristine lake trio: Bhimtal island aquarium, Sattal birding sanctuaries with 7 interconnected freshwater bodies, and 9-cornered Naukuchiatal for kayaking.

Pangot & Kilbury Birding

Perched higher than Nainital at 2,000–2,500m. Home to Cheer Pheasant, Koklass Pheasant, Himalayan Griffon, and pristine deodar walking trails.

Bhalugaad & Dhokane Waterfalls

Hidden forest waterfalls tumbling into crystal-clear turquoise mountain pools. Accessible via scenic 30-minute pine forest hikes.
