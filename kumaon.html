Cultural & Heritage Districts of Kumaon

Almora, Bageshwar, Champawat, and Udham Singh Nagar

Tier 2 • Cultural Capital Altitude: 1,638 m

Almora & Jageshwar Dham

Almora is renowned for its cultural heritage, Kasar Devi geomagnetic sanctuary on Crank's Ridge (frequented by Swami Vivekananda and Bob Dylan), Katarmal Sun Temple (9th century), Chitai Golu Devta Temple adorned with thousands of bells, and Jageshwar Dham (cluster of 124 ancient stone temples in dense deodars).

Jageshwar Dham Kasar Devi Binsar Sanctuary Katarmal Sun Temple Ranikhet Kausani
Tier 2 • Glacial Gateway Altitude: 1,004 m

Bageshwar & Baijnath

Perched at the sacred confluence of Saryu and Gomti rivers, home to the ancient Bagnath Temple. Historic gateway to legendary trekking routes into the Greater Himalayas: Pindari Glacier, Kafni Glacier, and Sunderdhunga. Nearby lies Baijnath, featuring 12th-century stone temples on the Gomti riverbank.

Bagnath Temple Baijnath Temples Pindari Glacier Kafni Glacier Kanda Valley
Tier 2 • Chand Dynasty Cradle Altitude: 1,610 m

Champawat & Abbott Mount

The ancient capital of the Chand Dynasty. Home to Abbott Mount with its colonial British-era stone cottages and cricket pitch, Mayawati Advaita Ashrama visited by Swami Vivekananda in 1901, the clifftop Purnagiri Shakti Peeth, and sacred Meetha Reetha Sahib Gurudwara.

Abbott Mount Lohaghat Mayawati Ashram Purnagiri Temple Devidhura Meetha Reetha Sahib
Tier 2 • Terai Plains & Lakes Altitude: 200 m

Udham Singh Nagar & Nanakmatta

The gateway to the Kumaon foothills, known for the holy Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib on the banks of Deoha river, where Guru Nanak Dev Ji meditated under the sacred Peepal tree, adjoining the vast Nanak Sagar Dam sanctuary.

Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib Nanak Sagar Dam Atariya Temple Drona Sagar
