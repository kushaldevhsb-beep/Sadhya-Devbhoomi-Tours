Sacred Pilgrimage • Top Level Section

CHAR DHAM YATRA

The ultimate Himalayan pilgrimage through Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath. Designed with gradual altitude acclimatization, verified biometric passes, and respectful Sattvic arrangements.

Origin of Yamuna
First Shrine • 3,293 m

Yamunotri Dham

Source of holy river Yamuna, Divya Shila, and cooking rice in boiling thermal waters of Surya Kund.

6 km trek from Janki Chatti (Pony/Doli available)
Descent of Ganga
Second Shrine • 3,100 m

Gangotri Dham

White granite temple where Goddess Ganga descended to Earth. Surrounded by Bhagirathi rapids and deodar woods.

Directly motorable highway up to temple courtyard
Supreme Jyotirlinga
Third Shrine • 3,583 m

Kedarnath Dham

Supreme Jyotirlinga in the glacial lap of Kedarnath Peak. Unmatched evening Aarti and profound bhakti.

16 km mountain trek or helicopter transfer from Phata/Sersi
Abode of Vishnu
Fourth Shrine • 3,133 m

Badrinath Dham

Abode of Lord Badri Vishal along the Alaknanda, holy bath in Tapt Kund hot spring, and Mana first Indian village.

Fully motorable road right up to Badrinath town
